Privacy Policy for UPPCS Decode

Last updated: September 2026

UPPCS Decode ("the App") is an educational syllabus-decoding and progress-tracking application designed for candidates preparing for the UPPSC PCS examination.

Information We Collect

UPPCS Decode does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or postal address.

The App does not collect personal information through registration, login, advertising, or analytics services.

Study Progress Data

The App allows users to mark syllabus micro-topics as completed. This progress information is stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of providing the application's progress-tracking functionality.

UPPCS Decode does not transmit this study-progress information to the developer or to a remote server.

Internet and Network Access

The core functionality of UPPCS Decode is designed to work offline. The App does not require an internet connection for its core syllabus and progress-tracking functionality.

Advertising and Analytics

UPPCS Decode does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to track users.

Third-Party Services

The App does not currently integrate third-party services that collect personal information from users.

Children's Privacy

UPPCS Decode is an educational application intended for examination preparation. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information.

Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer, no personal information is maintained by the developer through the App.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the functionality of the App changes. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or UPPCS Decode, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
