Privacy Policy for UPPCS Decode

Last updated: September 2026

UPPCS Decode ("the App") is an educational syllabus-decoding and progress-tracking application designed for candidates preparing for the UPPSC PCS examination.

Authentication Is Required

UPPCS Decode requires users to sign in before they can access the App's syllabus, progress-tracking, and other application features. There is no guest mode or option to continue without signing in.

Users may authenticate using an email address and password or through Google Sign-In.

Information We Collect

When you create or use an account, the App and its authentication services may process information associated with your account, including your email address and name or display name, where available. Authentication and account-related timestamps may also be maintained as part of the account record.

The App may also store your marketing communication preference when you provide or change that preference.

Study Progress Data

UPPCS Decode allows users to mark syllabus micro-topics as completed and track their study progress. Progress data is stored locally on the user's device for normal app operation and may be synchronized with the user's authenticated account using cloud services.

The App follows a local-first approach. Authentication is required for access, while progress tracking can continue using locally stored data. When network connectivity is available, relevant account and progress data may be synchronized in the background.

Firebase Services

UPPCS Decode uses Firebase services to provide authentication and cloud data synchronization. Firebase Authentication is used for email/password and Google Sign-In. Cloud Firestore may be used to store account-related information and synchronized study-progress data associated with the authenticated user.

Firebase services process data according to Google's applicable privacy and security practices. The App does not use Firebase for advertising or behavioral analytics.

Internet and Network Access

The App requires internet access for authentication and for background synchronization with cloud services when connectivity is available. After a user has successfully authenticated, locally stored syllabus and progress functionality is designed to remain usable when the device is temporarily offline.

Advertising and Analytics

UPPCS Decode does not display advertisements and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services to track users.

Data Sharing

The App does not sell personal information. Account and progress data may be processed by the Firebase services used to provide authentication and cloud synchronization, as necessary to operate the App.

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect account and application data. Access to cloud-synchronized user data is tied to the authenticated account and the application's access controls. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

UPPCS Decode is intended for examination preparation. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children below the applicable age for independent consent in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact the developer.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

Account-related and synchronized data may be retained while the account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App and maintain service records. Users may request or initiate account deletion through the account functionality provided in the App, subject to the application's verification and security requirements.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the App, its data practices, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or UPPCS Decode, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play Store listing.
